6. 0.6 mm NOZZLE?                                                                        [V] / [R]
  Sliced with the Bambu 0.6 mm profiles (0.30 mm layers, 2 walls, Arachne): openings still fine
  (smallest 4.74 mm). Times include a 5 mm brim (H2D 27 min 38 s, P1S 31 min 17 s); without the
  brim, subtract roughly 1.5 min. [R] Stay with 0.4 mm: the 0.30 mm layers leave visible steps
  on the curved surfaces, and saving 10-15 minutes on a 40-minute print is not worth it.

7. H2D-SPECIFIC                                                                         [R]
- Feed the PLA to the nozzle that your AMS 2 Pro is connected to. In the filament grouping
  dialog, set the mapping to manual if needed. (The test slice used extruder 1; time and
  toolpaths do not depend on which nozzle is used.)
- In the printer settings, set the nozzle flow type to match the nozzle actually fitted
  (verified slice: Standard 0.4 on both). For a High Flow nozzle, select High Flow and re-slice.
